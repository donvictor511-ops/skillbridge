# SkillBridge community guidelines

SkillBridge is a professional network for people building skills, careers, projects, and work relationships. These guidelines apply to posts, comments, communities, profiles, and marketplace activity.

## 1. Treat people with respect

Do not threaten, harass, target, or demean another person. Do not attack someone because of a protected identity or characteristic. Disagreement, constructive criticism, and firm discussion are allowed.

## 2. Keep the community useful

Share content connected to skills, work, learning, collaboration, and opportunities. Avoid repeated off-topic promotion, copy-paste spam, and disruptive posting.

## 3. Be honest about who you are and what you offer

Do not impersonate people or organizations, invent credentials, publish deceptive offers, or use SkillBridge to scam or phish members.

## 4. Respect privacy and consent

Do not publish another person’s private contact details, home address, identity documents, or private messages without permission.

## 5. Keep people safe

Do not promote violence, make credible threats, sexually harass members, or share exploitative sexual content. Report urgent safety concerns.

## 6. Respect other people’s work

Share work you own or have permission to use. Credit people when you use their ideas or materials.

## 7. Report concerns in good faith

Use report controls for specific content or behavior. Do not retaliate against members or organize false or repeated reports.

## Context and enforcement

A topic, identity, quoted slur, profanity, or negative opinion alone is not a violation. Reviewers should consider context, intent, reach, and likely harm. Respectful disagreement, criticism of work or organizations, discussion of difficult experiences, and educational discussion of harmful topics are allowed.

SkillBridge’s current automatic first layer only holds a third identical text post or comment from the same account within 24 hours. It is a spam signal, not a finding that a member intended harm. Held content is hidden until a human administrator approves or removes it. A removal or account restriction should include a clear reason; members can appeal suspensions and restrictions through the app. Review decisions are logged in moderation history.

## Keeping these rules maintainable

Keep this list short, behavior-focused, and consistent with the rules shown in the app. Update it when SkillBridge adds a new product area or a recurring harm cannot be handled under an existing rule. Avoid adding rules for every edge case; use context-based human review when the facts are unclear.
